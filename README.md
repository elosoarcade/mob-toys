# Mob Toys
**Mob Toys MCBE Add-On adds small collectible toys of the game's mobs, as well as a new trader called Seller, who sells the Toy Box, in which you can obtain the figures, along with many other sought-after items at quite reasonable prices.**

The Toy Box can only be opened with a golden pickaxe and will randomly drop one of the toys. Some, like the Zombie, are much more common, while the Glow-in-the-Dark Creeper and the Goat are the rarest.

There are a lot of figures available: Cow, Zombie, Sheep, Enderman, Witch, Creeper, Wolf, Pig, Polar Bear, Goat, Skeleton, Dungeons Creeper and Glow-in-the-Dark Creeper. More figures will be added in future updates.

_This addon is optimized to run effectively on Realms and dedicated servers with minimal hardware resource consumption and its available in english and español._ 🇬🇧 🇲🇽
